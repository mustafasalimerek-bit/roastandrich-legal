Community rules

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · These rules apply to the player chat in every Erek Studio game.

The chat is a place to talk with other players. Be someone people are glad to meet. There is zero tolerance for the following, and no warning is owed before we act on it:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
  • Hate or slurs against anyone for who they are.
  • Sexual content, or anything sexual involving minors in any form.
  • Scams, spam, advertising, or asking for money.
  • Sharing phone numbers, email addresses, links, or accounts on other apps in rooms or profiles, or anyone else’s details anywhere. In one-to-one chats, Chat+ members may share their own once the other person has written at least three messages, unless that person has turned it off.
  • Asking anyone for money, gift cards, codes or investments.
  • Pretending to be someone else, or using the chat if you are under 18.

What happens when a rule is broken

Messages are checked automatically when they are sent and again by an AI model. A message that breaks a rule is not delivered or is withdrawn, repeated breaks mute the account for 24 hours, and serious or repeated breaks end in a permanent ban. Every report is acted on within 24 hours.

Report and block

Tap the flag on any message or profile to report it; it disappears for you at once. Blocking someone hides you from each other and closes your conversation. Both are always free.
