Privacy Policy — Roast & Rich

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Developer: Erek Studio

Roast & Rich (“the app”, “we”) is a 3D coffee-roasting game for iOS and Android with an optional player chat. We designed it to be privacy-respecting: your gameplay runs and is saved on your device. This policy explains the limited data involved and your choices. The chat, and everything it involves, is described in its own section below; nothing in it happens unless you open the chat and sign in. The one exception is buying Chat+ without a chat account, described in section 2.

1. What we do NOT do

We do not run our own analytics. Outside the optional player chat (section 3) we do not collect your name, email, contacts, or precise GPS location. Your game progress (your wallet, the staff you have hired, the shifts you have completed and a short summary of each, purchases owned, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us; if you use the chat, only the two numbers on your chat card are (section 3). The only account is the optional chat account described in section 3.

2. Data handled by third parties

Advertising — Google AdMob. The free version of the app may show ads via Google AdMob, on iOS and on Android. To serve and measure ads, Google may process device and usage information such as a device/advertising identifier, coarse (non-precise) location, IP address, and ad-interaction events. This is controlled by Google, not by us. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases — Apple & Google. In-app purchases (Pro, and the Chat+ subscription described in section 3) are processed by the Apple App Store on iOS and by Google Play on Android. Payment details are handled by them, not by us; for Pro we only receive confirmation of what was purchased so we can unlock it on your device.

Chat+ bought without a chat account. You can buy Chat+ from the game’s NO ADS screen without signing into the chat. The app then sends proof of the purchase to our server — on iOS the App Store’s signed record of it, on Android the purchase token Google Play issues — and the server asks Apple or Google Play whether it is active and answers with its expiry date. The server stores nothing from that request; to limit abuse it counts requests under a keyed hash of the network address, never the address itself. The app keeps the confirmed status and its expiry on your device, so Chat+ works offline until that date and no longer, and checks it again when it renews. If you sign into the chat later, the purchase is stored with your account as described in section 3.

3. Player chat (optional)

From version 2.0, on iOS and on Android, Roast & Rich includes a player chat shared with other Erek Studio games, such as Orbion and Doltap: one server and one account for all of them. Nothing below happens unless you open the chat, accept the community rules, agree to the AI safety check, and sign in — with Apple on iOS, with your Google account on Android. You can play without it. The chat is for adults only: 18 or older, and 19 or older in Korea.

  • Sign in with Apple (iOS). We store only the identifier Apple gives us for your account. If Apple includes your email address, we do not store it.
  • Sign in with Google (Android). We store only the identifier Google gives us for your Google account. Google’s sign-in token also carries your email address, name and profile picture; we do not read, store or log them.
  • Sessions. Sign-in sessions are stored on the server only as hashes and expire after 90 days.
  • Your chat profile. A nickname (required) and your birth year (required, never shown to anyone, used only to confirm you are an adult). Optional: a display name, country, city, gender and the languages you speak, an avatar picked from a built-in set, and a short “about” line. Gender is never shown to other players; it is used only if you choose “only people of my gender can message me”. City appears only on your full profile.
  • Your Roast & Rich card. Next to your nickname, other players see how many shifts you have completed and your roastery’s level (1 on your own, 2 with an apprentice, 3 with a journeyman).
  • Messages. What you post in rooms and send in direct messages is stored on Cloudflare’s network so it can be delivered. Message text is deleted after 180 days.
  • Safety checks. Every message is checked by automatic rules and by an AI model from Anthropic (Claude), which classifies it for harassment, sexual content, threats, scams and sharing contact details. Anthropic processes the text only to return that classification and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. You are asked to agree to this, separately from the community rules, before you can use the chat.
  • Reports. If someone reports a message, we keep a copy of it and the five messages around it for up to 365 days to handle the report and meet legal obligations.
  • Profile photo (Chat+ only, optional). If you add one, the app shrinks it to 512×512 and removes its location and camera data before sending it. Before anyone can see it, it is checked by the same Anthropic AI model for nudity, children, violence, hate and contact details; a photo that fails is deleted at once. Approved photos are stored on Cloudflare’s network and shown on your chat profile while your Chat+ is active. Removing the photo or deleting your chat account deletes it.
  • Player rooms (Chat+). Chat+ members can create rooms of their own, public or private (entered with an invite code). We store the room’s name, optional description, language, owner, members and the people its owner removed. A room’s name and description are user content: they are checked by the same rules and Anthropic AI model as messages before a public room is listed, and reports about a room are handled like reports about messages. Anyone who can see a room can report it. A room with no messages for 14 days is archived. Player rooms are not available in Korea mode.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, we store your device’s push token — on Android, its Firebase Cloud Messaging registration ID — to tell you about new messages. Notifications are delivered by Apple’s push service on iOS and by Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android. A notification never contains message text or the sender’s name. A token that stops working is deleted.
  • Chat+. Chat+ is bought through the App Store on iOS and through Google Play on Android; we never see your name, email or payment details. If you are signed into the chat, our server confirms the purchase with Apple’s App Store Server API or with the Google Play Developer API and stores its identifier (on Android, the purchase token Google Play issues, and a hash of it), the product and the expiry date with your chat account, so Chat+ works in every Erek Studio game where you sign in with that account. On Android our server also acknowledges the purchase to Google Play, and Google Play tells our server when a subscription renews, is cancelled or is refunded; the server then reads its status again from Google. Buying Chat+ without a chat account is described in section 2.
  • On your device. If you use the chat or buy Chat+, the app keeps your chat sign-in tokens — on iOS in the Keychain, on Android in Android’s encrypted storage, left out of device backups — and the confirmed Chat+ status with its expiry date, so Chat+ keeps working offline until that date and no longer. Deleting the app removes them on Android; on iOS the Keychain may keep the sign-in tokens until you sign out or delete your chat account.

Other players see your nickname, display name, avatar or photo, country, languages, Roast & Rich card and what you post. They never see your birth year, gender, email, or Apple or Google identifier.

Korea mode. If your App Store or Google Play country, or your device region, is Korea, the chat runs in Korea mode, in every Erek Studio game: you see only Roast & Rich’s own rooms, there is no list of other players and there are no player rooms, and one-to-one messages are possible only between people who have both written in the same game room. Once an account is in Korea mode, it stays in Korea mode.

Service providers. The chat runs on Cloudflare’s network. For it, Erek Studio uses Anthropic for the safety checks described above, Apple for Sign in with Apple, purchase confirmation and push notifications on iOS, and Google for Sign in with Google, purchase confirmation and Firebase Cloud Messaging notifications on Android. Chat data is not used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your chat account: in the chat, open Settings → Delete chat account, or ask by e-mail as described on the account deletion page. Your profile, sign-in link, push tokens and message text are deleted immediately. Report copies described above may remain for up to 365 days. A Chat+ purchase record (transaction identifier or Google Play purchase token, product, expiry) stays without a link to any account, so the subscription can still be restored. Your Roast & Rich progress is not affected. Deleting the account does not cancel a Chat+ subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play › Subscriptions.

4. Your consent & choices

  • Europe/UK (GDPR): on first launch you are shown a consent choice for personalized ads. If you decline or consent is unavailable, ads are served in a non-personalized mode.
  • iOS (App Tracking Transparency): iOS asks your permission before any cross-app tracking. If you decline, ads remain non-personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android (advertising ID): you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.
  • Remove ads: Pro (monthly, yearly, or lifetime) or Chat+ turns off all advertising: from then on no ad is requested or shown, and on later launches the advertising SDK is not started at all, so no ad-related data is processed.

5. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If ads are shown, they are configured for a general audience. The player chat is for adults only: a birth year that does not show an adult closes the chat for that account, and the same Apple or Google sign-in cannot open a new one. Our child safety standards are on the community rules page.

6. Data retention

Because game data lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. Chat data is kept as described in section 3: message text for 180 days, report copies for up to 365 days, sign-in sessions for 90 days, and the rest until you delete your chat account (see the account deletion page for what goes at once and what stays). Data processed by Google/Apple is retained per their respective policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data and to withdraw consent. The personal data we hold ourselves is your optional chat account and what it involves (section 3): you can see and edit your profile in the chat, delete the account at any time in the chat (Settings → Delete chat account) or by e-mail as described on the account deletion page, and email us for a copy of your data or any other request. Requests about advertising data concern Google and Apple: you can change your ad-consent choice in the app (the PRIVACY button in the game, shown where your region requires it) and in your device settings, and contact us for help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected here. Version 2.0 rebuilds the game in 3D and adds the optional player chat and the Chat+ subscription, on iOS and on Android.
