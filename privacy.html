Privacy Policy — Roast & Rich

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Developer: Erek Studio

Roast & Rich (“the app”, “we”) is a coffee-roasting game with an optional player chat. We designed it to be privacy-respecting: your gameplay runs and is saved on your device. This policy explains the limited data involved and your choices. The chat, and everything it involves, is described in its own section below; nothing in it happens unless you open the chat and sign in.

1. What we do NOT do

We do not run our own analytics. Outside the optional player chat (section 3) we do not collect your name, email, contacts, or precise GPS location. Your game progress (shifts, records, purchases owned, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us; if you use the chat, only the number of shifts on your chat card is (section 3). The only account is the optional chat account described in section 3.

2. Data handled by third parties

Advertising — Google AdMob. The free version of the app may show ads via Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process device and usage information such as a device/advertising identifier, coarse (non-precise) location, IP address, and ad-interaction events. This is controlled by Google, not by us. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases — Apple & Google. In-app purchases (Pro, and the Chat+ subscription described in section 3) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details are handled by them, not by us; for Pro we only receive confirmation of what was purchased so we can unlock it on your device.

3. Player chat (optional)

Roast & Rich includes a chat shared with other Erek Studio games. Nothing below happens unless you open Chat and sign in. You can play without it.

  • Sign in with Apple. We store only the identifier Apple gives us for your account. If Apple includes your email address, we do not store it.
  • Your chat profile. A nickname (required) and your birth year (required, never shown to anyone, used only to confirm you are an adult). Optional: a display name, country, city, gender and the languages you speak, an avatar picked from a built-in set, and a short “about” line. Gender is never shown to other players; it is used only if you choose “only people of my gender can message me”. City appears only on your full profile.
  • Your Roast & Rich card. Other players see how many shifts you have completed next to your nickname.
  • Messages. What you post in rooms and send in direct messages is stored on Cloudflare’s network so it can be delivered. Message text is deleted after 180 days.
  • Safety checks. Every message is checked by automatic rules and by an AI model from Anthropic (Claude), which classifies it for harassment, sexual content, threats, scams and sharing contact details. Anthropic processes the text only to return that classification and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. You are asked to agree to this before you can use the chat.
  • Reports. If someone reports a message, we keep a copy of it and the five messages around it for up to 365 days to handle the report and meet legal obligations.
  • Profile photo (Chat+ only, optional). If you add one, the app shrinks it to 512×512 and removes its location and camera data before sending it. Before anyone can see it, it is checked by the same Anthropic AI model for nudity, children, violence, hate and contact details; a photo that fails is deleted at once. Approved photos are stored on Cloudflare’s network and shown on your chat profile while your Chat+ is active. Removing the photo or deleting your chat account deletes it.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, we store your device’s push token to tell you about new messages. A notification never contains message text.
  • Chat+. Purchases are made through the App Store. We confirm the transaction with Apple’s server and store its identifier, product and expiry date so Chat+ works in every Erek Studio game where you sign in. We never see your payment details.

Other players see your nickname, display name, avatar, country, languages, Roast & Rich card and what you post. They never see your birth year, gender, email or Apple identifier.

Deleting your chat account: Chat → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, sign-in link, push tokens and message text are deleted immediately. Report copies described above may remain for up to 365 days. Your Roast & Rich progress is not affected.

4. Your consent & choices

  • Europe/UK (GDPR): on first launch you are shown a consent choice for personalized ads. If you decline or consent is unavailable, ads are served in a non-personalized mode.
  • iOS (App Tracking Transparency): iOS asks your permission before any cross-app tracking. If you decline, ads remain non-personalized.
  • Remove ads: Pro (monthly, yearly, or lifetime) or Chat+ turns off all advertising, so no ad-related data is processed.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.

5. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If ads are shown, they are configured for a general audience.

6. Data retention

Because game data lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. Chat data is kept as described in section 3. Data processed by Google/Apple is retained per their respective policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data and to withdraw consent. The personal data we hold ourselves is your optional chat account and what it involves (section 3): you can see and edit your profile in the chat, delete the account at any time in Chat → Settings → Delete account, and email us for a copy of your data or any other request. Requests about advertising data concern Google and Apple: you can change your ad-consent choice in the app (the Privacy button, where offered) and in your device settings, and contact us for help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected here.
