This page is for Roast & Rich, a game by Erek Studio. Roast & Rich’s optional player chat (on iOS and on Android from version 2.0) uses a chat account, shared with Erek Studio’s other games that have the player chat, such as Orbion and Doltap. The game itself has no account. You can delete the chat account in the app, or ask us by e-mail if you no longer have the app.

In the app

Open Roast & Rich and tap PLAYER CHAT (the lower half of the home screen, or the big button at the bottom left of the game), then Settings → Delete chat account, and confirm. The account is deleted at once, as described below. Because the chat account is shared, this also deletes it in the other Erek Studio games where you use it.

By e-mail, without the app

E-mail mustafasalimerek@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Roast & Rich chat account”. Write your chat nickname and whether you signed in with Apple (iPhone or iPad) or with Google (Android). We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we delete it. We delete it usually within a few days and at the latest within 30 days of your request, and we tell you by e-mail when it is done. The deletion is the same as the in-app one.

What is deleted at once

  • Your chat profile: nickname, display name, birth year, country, city, gender, languages, avatar and “about” line.
  • Your profile photo, including the stored image.
  • The link to your Apple or Google sign-in, your sign-in sessions, and the push tokens of your devices (on Android, the Firebase Cloud Messaging registration ID).
  • The text of every message you sent, in rooms and in one-to-one chats; your one-to-one conversations close.
  • Player rooms you created (they close, and their names and descriptions are removed) and your place in other people’s rooms.
  • Your blocks, your Roast & Rich card in the chat, and the counters behind the daily message limits.

Your Roast & Rich game progress — your wallet, your staff, your shifts and any purchase — is not affected: it lives on your device and in your store account.

What stays, and for how long

  • Report copies. If someone reported one of your messages, we keep a copy of it and the five messages around it for up to 365 days, to handle the report and meet legal obligations. Then they are deleted.
  • Chat+ purchase records. The store’s transaction identifier (on Android, Google Play’s purchase token), the product and the expiry date stay without a link to any account, so a running subscription can still be restored.
  • Bans and age blocks. If the account was banned, or its birth year did not show an adult, a one-way hash of its Apple or Google identifier stays, so the same sign-in cannot open a new chat account. The hash cannot be turned back into the identifier.
  • An anonymous marker that a deleted account existed, with no profile or contact data, so other people’s closed conversations stay consistent.

Subscriptions

Deleting the chat account does not cancel a Chat+ subscription, and it does not touch Pro, which is not part of the chat account. Cancel a subscription in Google Play › Subscriptions on Android, or in your Apple ID settings on iPhone and iPad.

Other data

The game has no account and no online save: your progress stays on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. If you bought Chat+ without a chat account, our server stored nothing from that check. Advertising data for free players is processed by Google: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and on iOS you can turn off tracking for the app in Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.
